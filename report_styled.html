Executive Summary

In the last 48 hours, the platform has undergone a massive transformation, shifting from a standalone talent portal to a comprehensive **Professional Casting & Industry Marketplace**. We have successfully standardized the profiles of industry professionals (Photographers, MUAs, Coaches) and implemented advanced director-level casting management tools.

1. Director & Agency Casting Suite

Casting Management Evolution

  • Team & Collaborators: Managed invite-based system for production teams with role-based permissions (Admin, Editor, Viewer).
  • Unified Roles Inventory: Centralized tracking of all character roles across every production, including matching algorithm integration.
  • Applicant Lifecycle ATS: Enhanced Kanban pipeline with customizable folder management (e.g., "Shortlist", "Finalists", "Backup").
  • Billing marketplace: Subscription hub and one-off project promotions (Featured Listing, Urgent Hiring, Social Blasts).

2. Industry Professional Profile Overhaul

Professional Branding & Marketplace

  • Brand Identity: High-impact "Business Name" display and split-bio system (Elevator pitch vs. Detailed history).
  • Complex Pricing Models: Support for Hourly, Daily, Fixed, Package, and Custom Quote services.
  • Industry-Dynamic Data: specialized fields for Equipment Specs (Photographer), Kit Brands (MUA), and Delivery Modes (Coach).
  • Business Operations: Visibility for Studio Access, Insurance Availability, and NDA compliance status.
  • Work Availability: Integrated booking lead times and selectable working day preferences.

3. Security & Account Infrastructure

Verification & Protection Logic

  • Verification Guardrails: Strict `ProtectedRoute` logic enforcing `isVerified` status for professional tools.
  • Account Lifecycle Routing: automated handling of "Verification Process" and "Verification Pending" states for seamless onboarding.
